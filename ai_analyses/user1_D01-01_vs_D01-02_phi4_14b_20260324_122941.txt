Your sleep and activity patterns show some changes, including more light exposure during the day and a shift to earlier sleep times. Your overall activity levels have decreased slightly, but your daily routine has become more consistent.

- Your sleep timing is shifting earlier, but your social schedule hasn't changed much.

2) What looks good — keep doing this:
- Your daily routine has become slightly more consistent.
- You're getting better at reducing light exposure when you sleep.
- Sleep duration has increased, which is great for rest and recovery.

3) Your 7-day action plan:
- **Morning (8 AM):** Spend at least 30 minutes outside to get natural light.
- **Afternoon (2 PM):** Take a short walk or do some stretching indoors if it's cloudy.
- **Evening (6 PM):** Dim the lights and reduce screen time an hour before bed.
- **Night (10 PM):** Ensure your bedroom is dark by using blackout curtains or an eye mask.

4) How these patterns can affect you:
- More light during the day → Better alertness and mood → Reduced risk of feeling tired or down.

- Increased sleep duration → Enhanced memory and focus → Decreased risk of stress-related issues.
